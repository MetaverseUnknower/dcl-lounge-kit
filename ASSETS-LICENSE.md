## What's included

| Asset | From |
|---|---|
| Bar stools (pedestal in seven colourways, round, backrest), a couch, four tables | The Silt, by MetaPetal |
| Petal sofa, dark petal sofa, garden bench | MetaPetal HQ and Stellar Station, by MetaPetal |
| BETA and BLIP (robot bartenders), cocktail shaker | Stellar Station, by MetaPetal |
| Highball glasses | The Silt, by MetaPetal |

`packages/bar-kit/tools/build_drink_emote.py`) is Decentraland's and is not redistributed here: see that script for
where to get it.
