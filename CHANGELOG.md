# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-09-29)

First release, from MetaPetal's builds (The Silt, MetaPetal HQ, Galaxy Gardeners' Stellar Station).

### dcl-sittables

- Click-to-sit seats, with a glowing orb and a click box each, using Decentraland's own sitting emotes. Taken seats are
  shared between players and free themselves when their sitter leaves.
- `placeFurniture` and 17 ready models: nine bar stools, three couches, a garden bench and four tables.
- `onSitDown`, `onStandUp`, `setSitHandler`; `setSeatOrbStyle`.

### dcl-bar-kit

- Bartenders BETA and BLIP, with their models, and `Personality` for your own.
- A drinks menu with at-home recipes, and three mocktails (Helium-3 Fizz, Plasma Crystal, Mythic Bloom), with glasses
  and emotes.
- Drinks held in the hand and drunk standing, sitting or on a couch, seen by everyone; dropped on another emote,
  spilled on sprinting or jumping.
- "Would you like another?", and specials (menu items that run your code).
- Blender tools for the bartenders and the drinking emotes.
